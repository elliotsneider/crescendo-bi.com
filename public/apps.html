The Capture template

One reusable foundation.
Each app has one clear job.

Capture is Crescendo BI’s template for personal-data apps: secure accounts, records people can review and correct, portable exports, and limited access that can be revoked. Toss applies that foundation to meals; future apps can apply it to other kinds of data.
